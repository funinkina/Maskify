\textbf{VISION OF THE INSTITUTE}
\\
\\
JSS Academy of Technical Education, Noida aims to become an institution of excellence in outcome-based education by helping students build knowledge, skills, research aptitude, and ethical values for solving contemporary engineering problems.
\\
\\
\textbf{MISSION OF THE INSTITUTE}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Develop a platform for achieving globally acceptable levels of intellectual acumen and technological competence.
    \item Create an inspiring academic environment that supports quality research.
    \item Provide an environment that encourages ethical values and a positive attitude.
\end{enumerate}
\\
\\
\textbf{VISION OF THE DEPARTMENT}
\\
\\
``To spark the imagination of Computer Science engineers with values, skills, and creativity to solve real-world problems.''
\\
\\
\textbf{MISSION OF THE DEPARTMENT}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Inculcate creative thinking and problem-solving skills through effective teaching, learning, and research.
    \item Empower professionals with core competency in Computer Science and Engineering.
    \item Foster independent and lifelong learning with ethical and social responsibility.
\end{enumerate}
